# Knowledge Bases

Amazon Bedrock Knowledge Bases enables Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) by connecting foundation models to your data sources.

## Key Features

- Automatic chunking and embedding of documents
- Managed vector store or bring your own (OpenSearch, Pinecone, etc.)
- Supports S3, Confluence, SharePoint, Salesforce, and web crawlers
- Built-in citation and source attribution
