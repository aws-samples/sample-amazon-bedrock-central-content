## The scenario

In-house counsel has a vendor-supplied Master Services Agreement landing on their desk alongside an active negotiation deadline. The company's standard terms library lives in a shared MCP-indexed repository.

## Inputs

- Vendor-supplied MSA (PDF)
- Company standard terms (policy-library MCP server)
- Negotiation notes from procurement

## What the model did

Claude Cowork compared each clause of the MSA against the company's standard terms, surfaced the top deviations (liability cap, IP assignment, data-residency), proposed redline language that matched the company's drafting style, and identified two clauses that warranted partner-level escalation rather than a counter-draft.

## Outcome

Counsel received a clause-by-clause comparison, ready-to-insert redlines, and a short list of items to escalate — cutting the first-pass review from half a day to under an hour.
